Leadership

Service shaped by responsibility, youth development, and faith.

My leadership has grown through Scouting, the Order of the Arrow, church ministry, residence life, education, and camp service. Across those settings, I have learned to lead by organizing details, supporting people, communicating clearly, and helping young people grow.

I am especially drawn to leadership that combines practical problem solving with personal care. Whether I am helping with a camp program, supporting residents, teaching in a classroom, serving in ministry, or mentoring Scouts, my goal is to create structure that helps people succeed.
